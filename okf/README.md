# demo-okf

The wiki of Agroinsumos Ceiba, a **fictional** company for demos. Org_id `demo`.

Everything here is made up: the company, the three products, their doses and prices, the phone
numbers, the emails and the bank accounts. The banks are called "Banco Demo" and "Banco Ejemplo" on
purpose, so nobody can transfer money to a real account after watching a video.

The format is the same as any OKF bundle: `CLAUDE.md` has the map and `FORMAT.md` the format of a
page.
