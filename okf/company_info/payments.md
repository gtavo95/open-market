# Payments

There are two ways to pay.

## Payment link

This is the recommended way. It accepts Visa and Mastercard cards, credit and debit. The assistant
generates a personalized link for each purchase and the payment is made online, on the spot.

## Bank transfer

The accounts are in [bank-accounts.md](bank-accounts.md).

After transferring, the customer sends a photo or PDF of the receipt through the same channel where
they placed the order. The order is processed when the receipt arrives.

## Price in dollars

Catalog prices are in quetzales. If the customer asks for the price in dollars, it is the price in
quetzales divided by 7.8.

## Shipping is separate, unless the invoice is over Q1,200

If the invoice is not over Q1,200, when explaining how to pay it is said that shipping, Q40, is paid
separately: in cash to the courier on receiving the order, never in the payment link or the
transfer. It is said once. If the invoice is over Q1,200, shipping is free and it is not mentioned.
The amounts and the cutoff are in [shipping.md](shipping.md).
