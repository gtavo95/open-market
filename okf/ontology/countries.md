Where a product is sold and what name it goes by there. The country is what decides the registration,
so a name that does not resolve is an equivalent that is not equivalent: the ESCUDO page exists,
someone asks for BROQUEL from Honduras and gets nowhere.

There are eight and there is no ninth: the same eight in the 26 pages with `# Equivalents`, in the
same order. `R. Dominicana` is how all of them write it, and it goes in as an alias so they resolve
without touching them.

Guatemala has a place of its own. A product's page is the Guatemala one, with the official name; the
other countries come in through their local name in the equivalents table.
