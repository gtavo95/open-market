Where the product goes in. It changes the dose and the water volume, so it is not a decorative label:
Wetvane is 50 to 100 mL per drum foliar and 75 to 125 in drench, and reading a foliar rate as drench
under-recommends.

There are six and all are in the datasheets. `injection` is not here, even though the word appears:
both times it talks about the application equipment's injection system, not injecting the trunk.
`seed treatment` never appears either, and that is why it is not here.
